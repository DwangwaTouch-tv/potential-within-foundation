Who We Are

The World's Foremost Organisation for Youths

Potential Within Foundation (PWF) is committed to becoming a leading force in youth empowerment by unlocking the potential that exists within every young person. We believe that today's youth are tomorrow's leaders, innovators, and changemakers. Through mentorship, leadership development, emotional support, life skills training, and community engagement, we empower young people to overcome challenges, pursue their dreams, and create lasting positive impact in their communities.

Our vision extends beyond borders as we strive to build a generation of confident, resilient, and purpose-driven youth who are equipped to shape a better future for themselves and the world.

"POTENTIAL WITHIN FOUNDATION has more impact on the lives of youths. We work where others cannot, reach those who need us most, and leave behind systems that outlast any single programme."

Legal Basis

Our Mandate & Guiding Principles

The Potential Within Foundation (PWF) is mandated to empower young people in Malawi by providing emotional support, mindset transformation, leadership development, mentorship, life skills training, and community engagement. Our work is guided by a set of core principles that shape every aspect of the organization. We uphold integrity by maintaining honesty and ethical conduct, and accountability through responsible resource management and transparent decision-making.

We promote empowerment by creating opportunities for youth to realize their full potential, and inclusiveness by ensuring equal participation and respect for all individuals. Innovation drives our creative and problem-solving approaches, while teamwork fosters collaboration among staff, volunteers, and stakeholders.

We operate with respect for human dignity and diversity, and maintain transparency in all organizational activities to build trust and credibility. These guiding principles ensure that PWF remains committed to developing resilient, responsible, and purpose-driven young leaders who can positively transform their communities.

Core Principles

How We Work

  1. 01
    Youth Rights-Based Approach

    Every intervention is framed through the lens of Youth — not as charity, but as the fulfilment of legally recognised entitlements that governments have obligations to meet.

  2. 02
    Equity & Non-Discrimination

    We prioritise the most disadvantaged youth— those excluded by poverty, geography, gender, ethnicity, disability, or conflict. Reaching them is not an aspiration; it is our central organising principle.

  3. 03
    Do No Harm

    Every programme undergoes rigorous safeguarding assessment, and we maintain zero tolerance for any form of exploitation or abuse by staff or partners operating on our behalf.

  4. 04
    Neutrality & Independence

    In humanitarian settings, PWF provides services based solely on need, without favouring any side in a conflict. This principle protects our access to the most vulnerable youth in the most dangerous places.
